Who we are

More than a club.
A family.

The Crew MC was founded in Abu Dhabi in 2013 by a group of riders who wanted more from the weekend than a solo spin. More than a decade later we're still a small, tight-knit family — bikes, trucks and the people who love them.

We organise group rides right across the UAE, from the switchbacks of Jebel Jais to the dunes of Liwa, plus international road trips for our members.

Whatever you ride, if you share the passion, there's a seat at our table.
